## Charles Booth's poverty maps
A Victorian social cartographer who mapped London's inequality in 1889 by walking every street and giving each street a "class" designation.

Classes: <!-- .element: class="fragment" data-fragment-index="1" -->

- Lowest class. Vicious, semi-criminal. <!-- .element: class="fragment" data-fragment-index="1" -->
- Very poor, casual. Chronic want. <!-- .element: class="fragment" data-fragment-index="1" -->
- Poor. 18s. to 21s. a week for a moderate family. <!-- .element: class="fragment" data-fragment-index="1" -->
- Mixed. Some comfortable, others poor. <!-- .element: class="fragment" data-fragment-index="1" -->
- Fairly comfortable. Good ordinary earnings. <!-- .element: class="fragment" data-fragment-index="1" -->
- Middle class. Well-to-do. <!-- .element: class="fragment" data-fragment-index="1" -->
- Upper-middle and Upper classes. Wealthy. <!-- .element: class="fragment" data-fragment-index="1" -->
