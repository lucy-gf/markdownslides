### Complex driving factors

- We wanted to break this down into what we consider key drivers of these inequalities <!-- .element: class="fragment" data-fragment-index="1" -->
  - Age structure <!-- .element: class="fragment" data-fragment-index="1" -->
  - Underlying health conditions <!-- .element: class="fragment" data-fragment-index="1" -->
  - Vaccination coverage <!-- .element: class="fragment" data-fragment-index="1" -->
  - Social contact patterns <!-- .element: class="fragment" data-fragment-index="1" -->
- Each of these drivers interacts with another <!-- .element: class="fragment" data-fragment-index="2" -->
